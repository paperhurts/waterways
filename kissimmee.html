About this dataYearly means for each water year (October through September) at S-65E, where the river enters Lake Okeechobee. USGS gauged it from 1928 to 2004 (02273000). From 2014 the readings are SFWMD's, through the Corps of Engineers' CWMS: hourly at S-65E, plus the new spillway beside it, S-65EX1. Years with too few days of readings are left out.
