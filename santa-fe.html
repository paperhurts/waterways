About this dataAquifer maps before 2012 were contoured by USGS; later ones by FGS, using different wells and methods, at 10-ft intervals. Compare the broad shape between years. A difference of a few feet is within the noise. Flows are the May average for each year; values marked est. are scaled from the Fort White gauge. The Suwannee gauges below the Santa Fe have no daily record for these years, so they're left blank.
