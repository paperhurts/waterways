About this dataYearly means for each water year (October through September), from USGS daily records. East: the St. Lucie Canal below Port Mayaca, S-308 (02276877). West: the Caloosahatchee below Moore Haven, S-77 (02292000 through 2003, then 02292010 from 2008). South: the Hillsboro (02280500) and North New River (02283500) canals below S-351 and the Miami Canal at S-354 (02286400), added together in years all three report. The West Palm Beach Canal at S-352 isn't included: USGS stopped gauging it in 2008. Below zero, more water flowed into the lake than out. Years missing more than about a month of days are left out.
